# Privacy Policy

This document is a publication-ready draft for the Office AI Assistant add-in. Review it with legal counsel before a public marketplace submission.

## Data handled by the add-in

The add-in may process text selected by the user in Word or PowerPoint, text typed into the task pane, AI provider settings, and generated output.

## Direct mode

In direct mode, requests are sent from the user's Office task pane directly to the selected AI provider. API keys entered by the user are stored in the browser storage available to the Office task pane on that user's device.

## Server mode

In server mode, requests are sent to the deployed backend configured by the publisher. The backend may store user accounts, settings, chat history, and saved documents in its database. The publisher is responsible for hosting, securing, monitoring, and deleting that data when required.

## Third-party AI providers

When users choose an AI provider, submitted content may be processed by that provider according to that provider's own terms and privacy policy.
